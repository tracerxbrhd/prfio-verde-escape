# Verde Escape design

## Concept

Calm architectural hospitality: immersive forest photography, classic serif headlines, open margins, staggered cabin imagery and quiet green surfaces.

## Palette

Canvas #eeeee6; forest #283c2c; sage #748167; pale field #e1e5d9.

## Typography

Georgia serif for the editorial voice, Segoe UI/system sans for controls and copy. No external font requests.

## Spacing

8px rhythm, 60–105px section spacing; 40px mobile total gutters growing to 144px desktop gutters.

## Shapes

Square architectural frames, ruled facts and disclosures; circles reserved for image controls.

## Animation

One-second reveals and image scale, subtle desktop-only parallax, no autoplay gallery. Full reduced-motion alternative.

- [Codrops — Creative Hub / parallax and slideshow studies](https://tympanus.net/codrops/hub/)

## Interaction and access

Visible focus, semantic controls, labelled forms and native dialogs preserve keyboard behaviour. Selected controls communicate state through text or pressed state as well as colour. Layouts are exercised at 360, 768, 1440 and 2560 pixels. See QA.md for executed checks.
