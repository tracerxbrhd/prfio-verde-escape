BEFORE YOU WANDER

A few things
you might wonder.

How do we get there?

Both places are around 90 minutes from Berlin. The Lake House is closest to Bad Saarow station; the Woodland Suite is near Angermünde. Your stay guide includes the final route and local taxi information.

Can we bring our dog?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome in the Woodland Suite, with a €25 cleaning supplement per stay. Please keep them on a lead around wildlife. The Lake House stays pet-free.

What's included in our stay?

All linen, towels, firewood, coffee, tea, bicycles and your first morning's breakfast basket. The kitchen has everything needed for a simple meal. Nightly prices include taxes and standard cleaning.

Is there a minimum stay?

Two nights, so there is time to properly settle in. Arrival is from 15:00 and departure by 11:00. The planner supports stays of up to 21 nights.

How does the stay planner work?

Choose a cabin, dates and guests to calculate an indicative price and save a stay plan. This is an interactive booking demonstration: it does not check live inventory, accept payment or confirm a reservation.
